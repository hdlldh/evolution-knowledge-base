This is a tight, quotable, structurally distinct summary claim — essentially the chapter's own thesis statement in list form — that was not captured as its own claim/card in the extraction, though its component ideas appear scattered elsewhere.

The extraction's Section 2 (Concept 5) compressed this into a parenthetical list without the specific decision each profession faces, losing some of the rhetorical texture that establishes decision-making's breadth (mundane operational judgment calls, not just "big" strategic choices).
