## Missing Items

1. **The Norway women's team simulation (pp.175–176) — a first-person case, omitted.** Before the 2023 World
   Cup, Jordet ran a replica shootout with Norway's women: players were told their shots would be "rigorously
   dissected and analysed," and Jordet himself stood next to the spot to trigger the feeling of being judged.
   Captain Maren Mjelde: "your heart rate goes up… each player being put in the limelight… Even if there is
   nobody in the stands and zero consequences, you must rehearse somewhere… a lot of teams who end up in this
   situation probably regret that they did not prepare better." This shows that a *simple* replica shootout
   (not the full six-step protocol) already induces real pressure — a lower-tech complement the extraction lacks.

2. **"All competent penalty takers practise" + Kane's plan-and-rehearse detail (p.175).** Mourinho on Kane:
   "Harry decides before the game, a few days before, how he is going to take the penalty, and then spends 3-4
   days practising *that shot*." Plus Lewandowski ("Practice. Penalties after training a few times a week"),
   Ødegaard ("1-3 kicks… one penalty every day… against a goalkeeper"), and Mjelde ("five penalties the day
   before, five different shots, to feel what I was most comfortable with — the one I used, I'd scored in
   training the day before"). This is a distinct, actionable point (decide the specific shot in advance, then
   rehearse *that* one) the extraction under-develops.

3. **Two strong quotes.** Borges: "I haven't seen good penalty takers that don't practise" (the pithiest
   summary of the practice point). Seger, on her title-losing final miss: "I was fully sure I would score…
   the goalie doesn't even go in the right direction, but the ball went up. After this, it feels more or less
   as if my whole life falls apart" — a devastating window into the taker's certainty-vs-reality and the
   stakes. Both belong in §12.

4. **Minor colour:** South Korea losing to Germany (Ballack, 75') after the semi; Bev Priestman's "final as a
   natural aim." Non-essential.

## Overgeneralizations

- **Meta-analytic support rated "Strong."** Fair, but the 14-study meta-analysis spans sport *and policing*
  and the effect sizes/penalty-specificity vary; the extraction's §3 already notes this. Keep the caveat
  visible — none of the strong evidence is a real-shootout performance-improvement trial (the flagship
  penalty study validates pressure *induction* only, as §11 correctly flags).

## Important Nuance Lost

- That a *simple* replica shootout already induces pressure (Norway) — not only the elaborate six-step
  protocol — broadens the practical takeaway (you don't need the full apparatus to train the pressure).
- The "decide the specific shot days in advance, then rehearse *that* shot" nuance (Kane) — a concrete method,
  distinct from generic "practise more."
